## 🎮 Introduction

Whether you're a hardcore competitive player or someone who just wants to relax and have a good time, there's a place for you here.

## 📋 Server Rules

All members are expected to follow these rules to maintain a positive and enjoyable environment for everyone.

### General Conduct
1. **Respect All Players** - Treat everyone with courtesy and respect, regardless of skill level or background
2. **No Cheating or Exploiting** - Cheating, hacking, or exploiting game mechanics is strictly prohibited
3. **No Harassment** - Do not engage in bullying, toxic behavior, or any form of harassment
4. **Keep It Clean** - Avoid excessive profanity and inappropriate content
5. **No Spamming** - Avoid spamming in chat or other communication channels

### Gameplay Rules
6. **Fair Play** - Play fairly and follow all game mechanics as intended
7. **No Account Sharing** - Each account must be used by one person only
8. **Respect Game Assets** - Do not attempt to bypass security measures or damage server infrastructure
9. **Report Issues Properly** - If you find a bug or exploit, report it to administrators immediately
10. **PvP Ethics** - In competitive modes, engage in honorable combat and respect your opponents

### Community Engagement
11. **Positive Communication** - Use constructive language in all communications
12. **No Discrimination** - Discrimination based on any characteristic is not tolerated
13. **Admins Have Final Say** - Server administrators have the authority to enforce rules and make final decisions
14. **Rule Updates** - Stay informed about rule updates and changes

### Violations & Consequences
- **First Offense** - Warning
- **Second Offense** - Temporary Mute/Suspension (24-72 hours)
- **Third Offense** - Extended Suspension (1-2 weeks)
- **Severe Violations** - Immediate ban (cheating, harassment, etc.)

## 🏆 Ranking System

Our ranking system rewards active participation, skill development, and positive community contributions.

### Ranks & Tiers

| Rank | Title | Requirements | Perks |
|------|-------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | **Recruit** | Newly joined | Access to basic channels |
| 2 | **Novice** | 10 matches played | Access to tournament queue |
| 3 | **Adept** | 50 matches + 45% win rate | Custom role color |
| 4 | **Warrior** | 100 matches + 50% win rate | Early event registration |
| 5 | **Champion** | 250 matches + 55% win rate | VIP chat channel access |
| 6 | **Legend** | 500 matches + 60% win rate | Monthly special rewards |
| 7 | **Mythic** | 1000 matches + 65% win rate | Custom server perks |

### Points System

Players earn **Ranking Points** through:
- **Winning Matches** - +10 points per win
- **Streaks** - Consecutive wins multiply points (2x at 5 wins, 3x at 10 wins)
- **Event Participation** - +25 points per event
- **Community Contribution** - +5 points for helping other players
- **Attendance** - +2 points for each game night attended

### Seasonal Reset

- Rankings reset every **3 months** to keep the competition fresh
- Top 10 players each season receive special recognition and rewards
- Seasonal leaders are featured on our leaderboard hall of fame

### Leaderboard

Check your standing in real-time! The leaderboard displays:
- Current rank and tier
- Total ranking points
- Matches played
- Win/loss ratio
- Event participation count
